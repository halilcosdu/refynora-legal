Frequently asked

Why was my credit refunded?

When a fix can’t be completed for a technical reason, the credits spent are returned automatically. You’ll see the refund as a separate line in your credit history.

My purchased credits don’t show up.

Tap Profile › “Restore purchases”. If it persists, write to us with your App Store transaction number.

Where is my photo processed?

Only when you start a fix, it’s sent over an encrypted connection to our AI processing provider and deleted within 24 hours. Details in the Privacy Policy.

Where are my results saved?

In the Photos app on your phone, in the “Refynora” album. Nothing is stored on the server.

Please include

  • iPhone model and iOS version
  • App version (bottom of the Profile tab)
  • The tool where the problem happened, and a screenshot if you have one
