Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Refynora is an iOS app that fixes photos that came out badly. This policy explains which data is processed while you use the app and why.

Data controller

Halil Coşdu (Refynora). Contact: cosduhalil@gmail.com

No account

You don’t need an account to use Refynora. We never ask for or store your name, email address or phone number.

Data we process

  • The photo you pick. Only when you start a fix, that photo is sent over an encrypted connection to our AI processing provider abroad (Replicate, Inc., USA). No copy is kept on our servers; the provider’s copy is deleted within 24 hours. Refynora never uses your photos to train a model; processing at the provider is governed by the provider’s own terms. The fixed result is saved only on your phone.
  • Device attestation. Apple’s App Attest and DeviceCheck tie the free trial and your credit balance to your device. This is an anonymous, Apple-issued device identity; it isn’t linked to you.
  • Purchase records. When you buy a credit pack, Apple’s signed transaction is verified on our server and your credits are added. Card and payment details stay with Apple; we never receive them.
  • Notifications and usage. Our notification provider’s software (OneSignal, Inc., USA) starts when the app launches: it creates an anonymous subscription identifier and collects usage measurements such as sessions and notification interactions on its own servers. If you allow notifications, your device token is stored there too; you can withdraw that permission anytime in iOS Settings. None of this is linked to your identity or used for tracking.
  • Technical logs. Short-lived server logs (IP address, request time) are kept for error and security purposes and deleted automatically.

International transfers

Photo processing (Replicate, USA) and notifications (OneSignal, USA) run outside Türkiye. The app asks for your explicit consent before your first photo is sent abroad; you can withdraw it anytime in Profile › Consent. After withdrawal, AI tools ask again before running; on-device tools are unaffected.

Retention

  • Sent photos: at most 24 hours at the provider; never stored on our servers.
  • Credit ledger and device record: for as long as the service runs, and as long as accounting law requires.
  • Server logs: at most 30 days.

Your rights

Under Turkish data protection law (KVKK, article 11) and comparable laws, you can ask whether your data is processed and request correction, deletion or objection. Send requests to cosduhalil@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days.

Children

Refynora isn’t designed for children under 13 and doesn’t knowingly collect their data.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date above is updated and significant changes are announced in the app.
